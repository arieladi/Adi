# Parity checklist — the Export Audio/Video dialog, and what the Arrangement's selection renders

Drafted by win on 24 November for the window-fn seat, from the Live 12 manual's text (chapter 5, §5.1.3
Exporting Audio and Video, the PDF's pp. 122-127; with §3.21, pp. 58-59; §6.9, pp. 174-175; §27.4,
p. 522; §38.2.1, p. 960; §38.3.2 and §38.3.4, p. 964; §41.3, p. 986), for win to run against Live 12
(ADR-0108). Rows describe Live's behaviour in our words, never the manual's. ADI's side is EX-11 (the
Export page, `src/juce/ui_export.*`, `src/adi/ui/export_choices.hpp`, the window's flow in
`src/juce/ui_main.cpp`) and the engine's rows it waits on (EX-02 to EX-09, EX-06 for the dither), as
on the tree this sheet was drafted on (the page's comments cite manual 5.2.1, which is Exporting MIDI
Files; the dialog is §5.1.3). The checks are `exportPage` and the File menu's in `tests/test_ui_shell.cpp`
and the renderer's in `tests/test_render_queue.cpp`. The measures are AWAITING 24's readings (AWAITING
16 for the dialog's default type and depth). ADR-0205 d9's additions, below the page's divider, are
ADI's, not Live's, and are not this sheet's. The ADI column is headed "ADI today", not "ADI in this
PR": the sheet was drafted against the tree, not with a PR that builds the page. Status as in
`parity-7.2-shell.md`.
