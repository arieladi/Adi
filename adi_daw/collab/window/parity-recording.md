# Parity checklist — recording into the Arrangement

Drafted by win on 24 November for the window-fn seat, from the Live 12 manual's text: chapter 19,
Recording New Clips (pp. 405-417), chapter 21, Comping (pp. 424-429, only the take lanes a recording
makes), with §3.1 and §3.17 (pp. 34, 55-56), §17.1 Monitoring (p. 369), §17.2.1 (p. 370), §18.1 (p. 392)
and the shortcut tables of chapter 41 (pp. 999-1001). It is for win to run against Live 12 (ADR-0108).
The pages are the PDF's. Rows describe the behaviour in our words, never the manual's. Status as in
`parity-7.2-shell.md`. The ADI column reads the landing stack of 24 November: the window's loop recording
(`window-fn/loop-record`, CT-02's window half) and the snapshot's recording fields
(`window-fn/n64-snapshot-arm`, N64) are not in it. Recording into Session View slots is one row (47),
open while Session View is P2. ADI's own additions name their N row or ADR in the Manual / ADR column.
