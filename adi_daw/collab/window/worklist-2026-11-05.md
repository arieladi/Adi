1. what STEP-7-PLAN 7.2 to 7.6 still lists;

4. the parity passes.

dependency overrides that order where one exists. The Clip View (7.7) comes last because it waits
for the director to approve its picture.

**Groups.** Items are grouped by the files they mostly change, so one implementer can take a group
from top to bottom:

**Sizes.**
- S: one small PR.
- M: a PR across a few files, with its own parity rows.
- L: a PR that may want splitting in two.

**Every item** is a `window/<topic>` branch and PR cut from origin/main. Each one carries:
- its headless check;
- its parity rows, updated;
- a picture of ADI beside Live's in the PR body (LANES §3b item 1).
