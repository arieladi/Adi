# Walk B: the one session on Windows 10 (25 November 2026)

walk-B, a helper of win, by the director's order of 25 November 2026 (ADR-0248 d2). Companion of walk-A's
`2026-11-25-windows11.md` on `walk/windows11`.

## Setup

- Windows 10 LTSC 21H2 (build 19044), the director's lead PC, desktop free while he was away.
- Source: `origin/main` at 73e26e4aa, worktree of its own. Build: **Release** (optimised), Ninja + MSVC 19.44,
  `-DADI_WITH_JUCE=ON -DADI_WITH_PD=ON -DADI_WERROR=ON -DADI_ACCEPTANCE=ON`, target `adi_app` (the scanner is copied
  beside ADI.exe by the build), 2 jobs.
- ADI.exe started with `ADI_HOME` set to a new empty folder; the walk's projects in a scratch folder.
- Driving: no computer-use tools in this session, so the window is driven by synthetic mouse and key input
  (Win32 `SendInput` through pywinauto) with full-screen captures after every action.
- Hearing: the output is captured by WASAPI loopback of the device ADI plays to, and judged by its levels;
  the export is read back and compared with that capture.
- Hardware: the onboard HD Audio card and the monitor's HDMI audio. **No input is connected to the onboard
  card**: Windows reports its Microphone and Line In jacks as unplugged, and WASAPI lists no capture device.
  ASIO4ALL is installed. MIDI: a loopMIDI virtual port ("loopMIDI Port") was installed for the walk and
  notes are sent into it from a script.
- A known input signal: Voicemeeter (VB-Audio) was installed for its virtual cable; a script plays a tone into
  "Voicemeeter Input" and ADI records "Voicemeeter Out B1". The cable was checked first (tone in, -12.0 dBFS out).
- The output judged: the onboard card's **S/PDIF** output, captured by WASAPI loopback. The monitor's HDMI audio
  (Windows' default) is muted on this PC, and its loopback carries 1 to 2 LSB of noise even for a tone played to
  it directly, so it cannot be judged without unmuting the director's speakers.
