# Readings of Ableton Live 12: the right-click menus, 28 November 2026

**What this is.** The director drew automation on a track's volume by mistake, right-clicked the volume to
delete it, and got no menu. His word: "please make a sanity check on this, because also basic stuff from
Ableton". ADI behaves as Live 12 unless Live lacks the ability, so the first half of that check is Live's
own menus, read in Live itself. You have Live 12 on your machine and made the readings of
`collab/prompts/2026-11-27-live-readings.md`: the same rules hold here (a new Set, save nothing, change no
Setting, touch nothing in the library; no screenshot, machine name, user name or path in what you commit).

**Your report.** A new part, "Right-click menus", in `adi_daw/collab/basics/live-readings.md` on the branch
`basics/live-readings-log`, pushed after every few readings. For each reading:

| M | Where the right-click was made | The menu, top to bottom |
|---|---|---|

Write every entry in the menu's own words and order, a line `---` where the menu has a separator, `(grey)`
after an entry that is greyed out, `>` after an entry that opens a further menu (give that menu's entries
too, indented, when it has fewer than a dozen; for a longer one give the first three and the count), and
the key written beside an entry where one is shown. Where the menu changes with the state, read it in
each state named. Write "no menu" where Live shows none.

These readings come before the others still open in the earlier list: do them first.

## The readings

**The mixer's controls** (Arrangement View, an audio track and a MIDI track, the mixer section shown)

- M1. A track's Volume: with no automation; with automation drawn on it; while Automation Mode is on.
- M2. A track's Pan, the same three states.
- M3. A Send knob, the same three states.
- M4. The Track Activator; the Solo switch; the Arm button.
- M5. The meter; the Track Delay field; the In/Out choosers (Audio From, Audio To, Monitor).
- M6. The same controls on a return track and on Main (only what differs from M1 to M5); Main's Cue
  Volume and the Cue Out chooser.
- M7. The crossfade assign switches and the crossfader.

**Track headers and lanes**

- M8. An audio track's title; a MIDI track's; a group track's; a return's; Main's.
- M9. The empty area under the tracks ("Drop Files and Devices Here").
- M10. An empty place in a lane with only the insert marker; a time selection in one lane; a time
  selection across three lanes.
- M11. An audio clip; a MIDI clip; several clips selected at once.
- M12. An automation lane: an empty place; a breakpoint; the line between two breakpoints; a time
  selection in the lane.
- M13. A take lane and a clip in a take lane.

**The rulers and the Control Bar**

- M14. The beat-time ruler; the scrub area; the loop brace; a locator; the time ruler.
- M15. The Control Bar: Tempo, Time Signature, the Metronome, the Quantization chooser, the Follow
  switch, the Arrangement Position, the Loop switch, the Loop Start and Length fields, Punch-In and
  Punch-Out, the Draw Mode switch, the Automation Arm, Re-Enable Automation, Capture MIDI, Arrangement
  Record, Session Record, the CPU meter.

**Devices**

- M16. A device's title bar (an instrument, an audio effect, a MIDI effect).
- M17. A device's dial: with no automation, with automation, with a MIDI or key mapping.
- M18. A device's chooser (a menu of choices) and a device's switch.
- M19. The empty area of the Device View; the space between two devices.
- M20. EQ Eight: its display, a filter dot, and its title bar. Utility: the Gain dial.
- M21. A rack's title bar, a Macro, a chain in the chain list.
- M22. A plug-in device's title bar and one of its configured parameters.

**The Clip View**

- M23. The MIDI Note Editor: an empty place, a note, several notes selected, the piano roll's keys, the
  velocity lane, the loop brace and the markers above the notes.
- M24. The Sample Editor: an empty place on the waveform, a Warp Marker, a transient, the Start and End
  markers, the loop brace.
- M25. The clip's fields: the name, Start and End, Loop, the Warp chooser, Gain, Pitch, the Envelopes
  choosers.

**The Browser**

- M26. A label in the left column (Sounds, Drums, a Collection, Places' User Library, a folder you
  added); the empty area of the left column.
- M27. In the content list: a sample, a preset, a device, a plug-in, a folder, a Set, a MIDI file; the
  Name column's header.
- M28. The Preview area and its switch.

**The rest**

- M29. The Info View; the Status Bar; the overview at the top of the Arrangement.
- M30. Session View: an empty slot, a clip, a scene, a track's Clip Stop button, a track title there.
