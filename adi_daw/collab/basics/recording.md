## For the director

- SC-22. The shared WASAPI cable reported a 20 ms round trip. After that correction the clicks remained about 100–110 ms late. I recommend taking the reported-offset fix and keeping Driver Error Compensation and a repeat cable measurement under SC-255.
- R45 / SC-245. Monitor Off continues to play the earlier clip over the capture. I recommend leaving that named policy in place until the listening check confirms whether the older clip is muted at the punch point. [Live 12 monitoring and recording](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/recording-new-clips/)
- SC-23 / SC-261. The routing session reports its own SC-23 work in the In/Out chooser branch. I recommend one external-input chooser on the landing stack, with the physical channel identifiers and Windows recording and meter regressions retained alongside routing's wider chooser coverage. [Routing report](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/basics/routing-log/adi_daw/collab/basics/routing.md)
- SC-25. ADR-0132 d1 keeps long recordings in one file by promoting WAV to RF64 in place. I recommend keeping that container and the three tested bit depths. An alternate recording container needs a decision on how a take continues beyond its size limit.
