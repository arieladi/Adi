# The sanity check of 26 November 2026

**Why it exists.** On 26 November the director saw that ADI draws no waveform while audio records, and
that the plan had no row for it. He ordered a check of the whole program for other basic DAW behaviour
that is missing and unplanned. His rule from that day: **the basics come first.** Every gap below is a
fix under ADR-0248 d1 and is worked now, whether or not the hold has ended (ADR-0257 d1).

**The register.** This paper is the register of its own rows (DF-W59): round 1's, SC-01 to SC-112, and round 2's, SC-120 onward, in the second part. A row is struck here, with its pull request, when its fix is on main.

**How it was made.** Six audits of main at `5ebc7453f`. Each wrote its own list of basics from general
DAW practice and Live 12, before reading ADI's documents, and then traced every item to the window's
code, the engine, the plan and the register. One audit asked only what the screen should show. Nothing
was run in the window: a state rests on reading code, tests and the two walk reports of 25 November.
Two items were then checked by test and are marked.

**How the gaps slipped.**
- `docs/FEATURES.md` ticks a row when the file format can store it, not when the feature works. Punch
  in and out carries that tick.
- The parity sheets hold rows marked "open" that no plan or register row picks up, and some are stale.
- Every list ADI kept was a list of commands. None listed what a musician expects to see.
- Settings shows about 160 controls that nothing reads, and thirteen Control Bar buttons answer "not
  built yet".

**States.** *Missing*: not built and owned by no row. *Engine only*: built, with no way to reach it in
the window. *Late*: owned by a row, placed too late for how basic it is. *To run*: read in code as
suspect; needs one run. A row id in the last column is the owner that already existed.

**Waves.** 1 = the weekend of 27 to 29 November, in a queue of `collab/prompts/2026-11-27-*.md`.
2 = the week after. 3 = stays with its row.

## Round 2: nineteen systematic audits (26 November 2026)

**Why a second round.** Round 1 worked from each auditor's own idea of the basics. The director asked
for a deeper one, so that every hole is closed or at least planned. This round is systematic: each audit
took one source and went through it completely.

**How it was made.** Nineteen audits of main at `82bb5c389`. Eleven read chapters of Live 12's manual
against ADI; two listed every control of ADI's own window and asked of each whether it does what its
label says; six asked one cross-cutting question (a new user's first hour, gestures and right-click
menus, audio devices and files, export and import, plug-in hosting, undo and the safety of the work).
They wrote 449 rows. Merged, these are the 287 new rows below (SC-120 to SC-406), notes on 43 rows of
round 1 (written into those rows), and 11 lines that a decision already settles. Nothing was run in the
real window. "Measured" and "confirmed by a probe" mean that a headless program was built against main's
libraries and run; "read, not run" rests on the code alone; "To run" names the one run that settles the
row. The pointers come from audits: confirm the fault first.

**Columns.** *Grade:* A = a new user meets it in the first hour; B = in the first week; C = now and
then, or advanced. A row carries the highest grade any audit gave it. *State:* as in round 1, and three
more. *Partial:* it works, but differs from Live in a way a user notices. *Dead control:* shown, and
does nothing. *Wrong:* a defect. *Where:* where to start reading, relative to `adi_daw/`. *Row:* the
plan, register or sanity row that owns it today, or "new". "Beside SC-nn" names the round 1 row it
stands next to. "Moves up" means its owner exists and is placed too late for how basic it is.

**Queues.** The six of round 1, and eight new ones, each one family of files: *browser* (search, places,
folders, preview, labels), *plugins* (the plug-in host and its windows), *safety* (undo steps, backups,
recovery, notices of missing media), *edit* (selection, right-click menus, clipboard and keys in the
Arrangement), *device* (audio and MIDI devices, file formats), *export*, *automation*, *routing* (the In
and Out choosers, MIDI out, take lanes).

**Waves.** 1 = grade A, and a defect of any grade that loses work or stops a first-hour scenario. 2 =
the other grade B rows, and every dead control. 3 = grade C, and a row that stays with its plan row.
