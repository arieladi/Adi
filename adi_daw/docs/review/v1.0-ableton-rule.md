# The Ableton rule applied to the remaining decisions (v1.0 review)

The director's standing rule of 4 October 2026: always the Ableton way, unless Ableton lacks the ability, in which case ADI adds.

## 1. Outcomes the rule settles (one confirmation)

### Mixer strip, pan, solo and sidechain taps (sections 8.1 and 8.3)

- **A3-15:** The mixer paragraph goes in, with the pan-law sentence you approved (B2-18). ADI gets Live's Split Stereo Pan Mode: two sliders that place left and right separately. Width and the two phase switches stay in Utility, as in Live, not on the strip. A converted project that stores a strip width or phase switch is named as a problem, never ignored in silence.
- **B2-17:** Solo is exactly Live's. With Solo in Place on, a soloed track keeps its returns, so a soloed vocal keeps its reverb. A soloed return still hears what other tracks send to it. A compressor keeps ducking under solo when its trigger is taken before the mixer. ADI also gets Live's Solo/Cue switch, Cue Out and Cue Volume. Exclusive Solo is already in ADI, on, as Live.
- **B4-01:** Folded into A3-15 and B2-17: one paragraph for all three. It supplies the true sentence that input gain is applied and the track delay plays. Nothing new to decide.
- **A3-25:** A sidechain, and track-to-track routing, get Live's three tap points: Pre FX, Post FX and Post Mixer. Only Post Mixer follows the fader, mute and solo, so a silent 'ghost kick' still triggers a compressor from the two early points. The tap chooser sits under the source chooser, as Live lays it out. Today ADI plays Post Mixer only.
- **A3-12:** The track delay is built. It is called Track Delay and is shown and typed in milliseconds, as Live. ADI gains Live's Delay Compensation on/off switch in the Options menu, and Track Delay is unavailable while that switch is off, as in Live.

- **sends-choice-4:** ADI's own mixer makes only Live's sends: to return tracks, one knob per return, no pan on a send. Offering sends to any track, as REAPER does, is out.

**sends-choice-2 · Freeze Group and what its tracks were sending to the returns**
