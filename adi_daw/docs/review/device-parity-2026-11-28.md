## SC-481 — ADI-Shaper and Cableguys ShaperBox 3.6

The clones build session measured **Cableguys ShaperBox 3.6**. The build log names the tested version, distinguishes it from the published v3.6.3 manual, and records **eleven Shapers** plus the separate **Compressor and Oscilloscope** tools. The v3.6 manual’s introduction says nine. Its contents and individual chapters enumerate eleven Shapers. Their names are Time, Pitch, Drive, Noise, Reverb, Filter, Liquid, Crush, Volume, Pan and Width. The 3.6 measurement contract confirms all eleven. [Build session reference and method](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/collab/clones.md#L1715-L1719), [first acceptance cases](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/collab/clones.md#L1795-L1808), [Cableguys manual, v3.6](https://downloads.cableguys.com/Cableguys-ShaperBox-3-Manual.pdf?v=3.6.1#page=2).

Main declares **418 controls** and processes **seven stages**, in order from Volume through Width, Pan, Filter, Sweep, Drive and Crush. Sweep is ADI’s name for the reference LiquidShaper’s flanger/phaser stage. A band has eight fixed values per contour and a shared mix and clock. Filter, Sweep and Crush each have two independent contours. The opened view in sheet 6 shows stage tabs, crossovers and the editing graph. The face shows a single selected stage and band with the large Master Mix. Placements below extend those rules for additional controls. [Pd parameter declarations](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/pd/devices/ADI-Shaper.pd#L11-L846), [core controls and contours](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/src/adi/dsp/adi_shaper.hpp#L25-L54), [render order](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/src/adi/dsp/adi_shaper.cpp#L790-L796), [ADR-0248 d11 and d14](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/docs/DECISIONS.md#L19388-L19395), [device sheet 6](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/b65fb0bac200689a9464168932e6e8731517ef4c/adi_daw/collab/window/design/proposals/devices-6.html#L23-L64).

### Sound comparisons on the seven implemented Shapers

The build session ran registered ADI-Shaper and ShaperBox 3.6 on the same stereo kick-and-bass samples at 48 kHz and 120 BPM, with editors closed. The seven-stage review measured four-second captures at 256 and 2,048 frames per callback after 98,304 warm-up frames. The session also used impulses, steady tones and moving shapes to isolate individual settings. The errors below belong to the named fixtures and build revisions. [Seven-stage review and measurement setup](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/collab/clones.md#L1153-L1175) · [Registered main comparison](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/collab/clones.md#L729-L747)

- **Volume.** On identical 48 kHz kick and bass, three bands at gains 0.35, 0.70 and 0.85 with 12 and 24 dB/octave splits had a maximum sample error of **0.000000477** over 192,000 frames per channel at each of 64, 256 and 1,024 frames per callback on the five-stage build. The held one-bar gate in the original three-stage build changed **16 samples earlier** than the reference and agreed within **0.00001** after shifting those edges. The first-order split repair now in main reduced the three-gain kick-and-bass error from **0.003218** to **0.000000021 RMS** on its own fixture. [Three-band gain test](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/collab/clones.md#L1645-L1649), [gate](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/collab/clones.md#L1681-L1691), [first-order split repair](https://github.com/arieladi/Adi/blob/a916c47c1d1e01aee9c70824e95c3e592a35c3c0/adi_daw/collab/clones.md#L181-L187).
